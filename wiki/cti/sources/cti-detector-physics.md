# Sources: CTI detector physics

Foundational papers on the silicon-level physics of charge trapping —
Shockley–Read–Hall theory, trap species in p- and n-channel CCDs,
radiation-induced trap creation, and electrode-level modelling.

## Shockley & Read 1952 — recombination theory

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Shockley1952`
**Reference:** Statistics of the recombinations of holes and electrons; doi:10.1103/physrev.87.835; Physical Review
**Concepts:** [[ccd-trap-physics]]

**Supports:**
- Establishes the Shockley-Read statistical treatment of hole/electron recombination in semiconductors.
- Provides the foundational trap-mediated recombination framework later used in Shockley-Read-Hall detector models.

**Use when:**
- Citing the semiconductor trap-recombination foundation underlying CTI trap-emission models.

**Do not use for:**
- Mission-specific CTI calibration, CCD readout algorithms, or Euclid VIS trap-pumping results.


## Hall 1952 — trap theory

**Canonical BibTeX key:** `Hall1952`
**Reference:** Electron-hole recombination in Germanium; doi:10.1103/physrev.87.387; Physical Review
**Concepts:** [[ccd-trap-physics]]

**Supports:**
- Reports trap-mediated electron-hole recombination in germanium, the empirical companion to the Shockley-Read statistics.
- Completes the Shockley-Read-Hall recombination framework that CTI trap-emission models build on.

**Use when:**
- Citing the Hall side of Shockley-Read-Hall theory as the foundation of CCD trap-emission physics.

**Do not use for:**
- Mission-specific CTI calibration, CCD readout algorithms, or Euclid VIS trap-pumping results.
